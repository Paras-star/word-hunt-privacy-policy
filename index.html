Privacy Policy

Word Hunt: Mystery Dumplings

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Hunt: Mystery Dumplings ("the App") handles information when you use the game. We aim to keep the App simple and privacy-conscious. The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to play.

1. Information handled by the App

The App stores gameplay information locally on your device, such as game progress, unlocked content, coins, hints, and related game settings. This information is used to provide the game's functionality and is not intended to identify you personally.

The App does not operate its own user account system, advertising database, or server-side player profile.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertising, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, app interaction, and advertising-related information in accordance with their own policies and the choices available to you.

Rewarded advertisements are optional. The App only grants an in-game reward after the advertising SDK reports that the required rewarded-ad event has been completed.

For more information about how Google processes information, please see Google's Privacy Policy .

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required by applicable law or Google's policies, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage advertising consent.

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be shown a consent message before advertising is requested. You can manage or withdraw applicable privacy choices through the App's Privacy Choices option when it is available.

Advertising requests are gated by the consent status provided by the applicable consent framework. If advertising cannot be requested, the game remains playable without ads.

4. Third-party services

The App uses third-party services necessary for advertising and related consent management. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

  • Google AdMob — advertising and advertising measurement. Google partner policies
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — consent and privacy-choice management for applicable users.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as part of the advertising services described above. The scope of that processing depends on your location, consent choices, applicable law, and the settings used by the advertising service.

6. Data retention and deletion

Gameplay data stored locally by the App remains on your device until it is removed by the App, cleared through your device's app-data controls, or the App is uninstalled, subject to the behavior of your operating system.

We do not maintain a personal player account or a server-side database containing your gameplay profile.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not intended to collect personal information from children. The App does not require users to submit personal information to play. Advertising and consent behavior may vary based on applicable age, audience, and legal requirements.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights relating to access, deletion, objection, restriction, or withdrawal of consent.

For advertising consent choices, please use the App's Privacy Choices feature when available. For questions or privacy requests relating to this App, contact us using the address below.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our services, or applicable legal requirements. The updated version will be posted at this page with a revised effective date.
